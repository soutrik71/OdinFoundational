Classic Lasagna

Description

Classic lasagna is a hearty baked pasta dish layered with savory meat sauce, creamy cheese, and tender noodles. It is a filling meal that works especially well for family dinners or gatherings where you want something warm and satisfying.

Although it takes a little more assembly than some recipes, the process is straightforward. Once baked, the layers come together into a rich and comforting dish that is well worth the effort.

Ingredients

  • 9 lasagna noodles
  • 1/2 pound ground beef
  • 2 cups marinara sauce
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon dried parsley
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Boil the lasagna noodles according to the package directions, then drain and set them aside.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan, cook the ground beef until browned, and season it with salt and black pepper.
  4. Stir the marinara sauce into the cooked beef and let it simmer for a few minutes.
  5. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, parsley, and half of the Parmesan cheese.
  6. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish, then add a layer of noodles.
  7. Top the noodles with some ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used, then finish with mozzarella and the remaining Parmesan.
  9. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, let it rest for 10 minutes, then slice and serve.
