Homemade Cheese Pizza

Description

This homemade cheese pizza is a simple and comforting recipe with a crisp crust, rich tomato sauce, and plenty of melted mozzarella. It is a great choice for an easy family dinner or a fun weekend cooking project.

The recipe uses basic ingredients and comes together in a few clear steps. Once baked, the pizza has a golden crust and a cheesy topping that makes it both flavorful and satisfying.

Ingredients

  • 1 prepared pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 2 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried basil
  • Pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Roll or stretch the pizza dough into a circle and place it on a baking tray or pizza pan.
  3. Brush the edges of the dough lightly with olive oil.
  4. Spread the pizza sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edge.
  5. Sprinkle the mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses over the sauce.
  6. Season the top with oregano, basil, and a small pinch of salt.
  7. Bake the pizza for 12 to 15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbling.
  8. Let the pizza cool for a few minutes, then slice and serve.
